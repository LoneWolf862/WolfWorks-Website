Wolfs Stickers Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Wolfs Stickers handles user information.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Overview

Wolfs Stickers is an iMessage sticker pack published by WolfWorks Labs. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information Collection

Wolfs Stickers does not collect:

  • Names or contact information
  • Email addresses
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Messages or message contents
  • Photos, files, or other user-generated content
  • Advertising or tracking information

How the App Works

The sticker images included with Wolfs Stickers are stored within the app and are made available through Apple Messages. The app does not operate external servers and does not transmit user data to WolfWorks Labs or any third party.

Accounts and Login

Wolfs Stickers does not require an account, login, subscription, or registration.

Analytics and Advertising

Wolfs Stickers does not use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party marketing tools.

Third-Party Services

Wolfs Stickers operates through Apple Messages and the Apple operating system. Apple may process information according to Apple's own privacy policies and platform terms. WolfWorks Labs does not receive personal information from Apple through this app.

Children's Privacy

Wolfs Stickers does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of Wolfs Stickers introduces features that collect or process user information, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to WolfWorks Labs.
