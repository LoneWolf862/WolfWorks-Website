Engineering Portfolio

Wolf's Works

This site is a project portfolio dedicated to documenting and cataloging my chronically expanding list of interests through an absurd, ever-accumulating assortment of projects, prototypes, and perpetual problems in practical engineering, including but not limited to: Custom PCBs, Control Systems, Automation, Instrumentation, Robotics, and Embedded Hardware/Software.

This site is not a product or a polished formal portfolio, it started as a fun side project and it still is. It's a place where I can be less formal, document projects as they happen, explore new ideas, and show more personality, without doxing myself. Although I welcome potential employers, partners, and contributors, think of it as the equivalent of an open workshop rather than a showroom.

This site is a place for me to share the things I make, the lessons I learn, and hopefully pass a little of that knowledge along to inspire others to build something of their own.
